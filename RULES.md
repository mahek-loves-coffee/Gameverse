# The Gameverse — Game Rules & Room Limits

## Room sizes

| Game | Minimum | Maximum | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| UNO Normal | 2 | 8 | Standard mode; no draw stacking |
| UNO Flip | 2 | 8 | 112 double-sided cards; light/dark faces switch on Flip |
| UNO No Mercy | 2 | 8 | 168-card No Mercy deck; 8-player cap is a Gameverse house extension |
| Ludo | 2 | 4 | One token set per seat |
| Chor Ko Dhundo | 4 | 4 | Raja, Mantri, Sipahi, Chor |
| Sola Parchi | 4 | 8 | Each added player adds one complete fruit set |

## UNO Normal

- Seven cards per player. Match the top card by colour, number, or symbol; wild cards choose a colour.
- Draw one when unable to play. If that drawn card is playable, it may be played immediately; otherwise pass the turn.
- Draw penalties are resolved by drawing; stacking is not enabled in this mode.
- Wild Draw Four is blocked when the player still has a card of the active colour.
- A player reaching one card can call UNO. Other players can catch an uncalled UNO before the next turn begins; caught player draws two.

## UNO Flip

- Double-sided 112-card deck. Each card has a Light face and a Dark face.
- A Flip card changes every player's hand, the draw pile, and discard pile to the other face.
- Light side includes Draw One and Wild Draw Two; Dark side includes Draw Five, Skip Everyone, and Wild Draw Colour.
- The same number remains on both faces; colours and action values change with the side.
- UNO call/catch is supported. Draw penalties are not stackable in this mode.

## UNO No Mercy

- 168-card deck, seven cards per player.
- Stacking: answer a draw penalty with a draw card of equal or greater value; otherwise take the full accumulated penalty and lose the turn.
- A 7 requires a hand swap with a chosen opponent. A 0 passes every hand in the current direction.
- Skip Everyone grants the current player another turn. Discard All removes every card of the matching colour from the player's hand.
- Wild Reverse Draw Four reverses direction and applies the draw penalty; Wild Draw Six, Wild Draw Ten, and Wild Colour Roulette are included.
- If unable to play, draw until a playable card is found. A player with 25 or more cards is eliminated.
- The official Mattel instruction sheet describes the physical game for 2–6 players; Gameverse keeps the requested 2–8 room range as a house extension.

## Ludo

- Roll a six to bring a token out of the yard. Move by the rolled number; an exact count is required to reach home.
- Three consecutive sixes cancel the third move and pass the turn.
- Landing on an opponent's non-safe square captures that token and grants a bonus turn. Reaching home also grants a bonus turn.
- First player to bring all four tokens home wins.

## Chor Ko Dhundo

- Exactly four players receive one private role each: Raja, Mantri, Sipahi, and Chor.
- All players must privately reveal that they have viewed their role before the Sipahi can lock a guess.
- Scoring: Raja 500, Mantri 200, correct Sipahi guess 100; if the guess is wrong, Chor receives 100.

## Sola Parchi

- Every fruit has exactly four matching chits. Four players start with 16 chits; each extra player adds four matching chits of a new fruit.
- Each player starts with four chits. On a turn, pass one selected chit clockwise: sender has three until a chit reaches them; receiver temporarily has five until passing one onward.
- First player to hold all four chits of the same fruit wins.
- Fruit chits use original vector illustrations rather than emoji glyphs.
